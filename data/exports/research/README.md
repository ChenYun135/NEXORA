# Public research exports

This directory contains aggregate, public-safe and reproducible research metadata. It intentionally excludes private research workspaces, manuscripts, restricted microdata, credentials and local paths.

- `model-v2-variables.csv` — declared public variable registry.
- `policy-instruments.csv` — scenario instrument schema, not observed policy effects.
- `provider-registry.json` — public provider planning metadata.
- `figures/` — deterministic SVG research diagrams.

These artifacts are specifications or aggregates, not forecasts or causal estimates.
