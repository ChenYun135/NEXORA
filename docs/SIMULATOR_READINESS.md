# Simulator Readiness / 模拟器就绪度

Sprint 9 is implemented as a transparent, conceptual research model. The model, parameter registry, annual Euler solver, ten bilingual prototype scenarios, one-way sensitivity analysis, deterministic exports, validation tests, and `/simulator` interface are present.

Readiness status: `PARTIALLY_CALIBRATED`. Research Capacity is observed-calibrated from mapped OpenAlex publication counts; Technology Knowledge Stock is derived-calibrated from the same series. Seven other stocks remain assumption-led. Production patent, capital, startup, talent, adoption, infrastructure-effect, policy-effect, metro, and longitudinal network data are unavailable and are never replaced with zero or Demo values.

The simulator explores conditional trajectories; it is not a forecast, probability estimate, policy recommendation, or oracle. Its results must retain the `SIMULATED` label and the model, equation, solver, scenario, evidence-snapshot, and parameter versions.

See `SIMULATOR_INPUT_READINESS.md`, `SYSTEM_DYNAMICS_MODEL.md`, `SIMULATOR_CALIBRATION.md`, `SIMULATOR_MODEL_VALIDATION.md`, and `SIMULATION_LIMITATIONS.md` for the auditable boundary.
