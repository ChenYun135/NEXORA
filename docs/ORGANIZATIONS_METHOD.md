# NEXORA Organizations Method v0.6

The Sprint 6 dataset is a structured bilingual demonstration. Its 65 fictional organizations, activities, relationships, signals, and metrics are not asserted real-world facts. The interface keeps four layers distinct:

1. Entity identity — Verified Public Entity or Demo Entity.
2. Profile metadata — source-backed fields or explicitly Demo-configured fields.
3. Relationship claims — evidence-linked edges, separately marked observed/derived/Demo.
4. Derived metrics — connectivity is relationship count, diversity is distinct relationship types, and evidence coverage is satisfied evidence dimensions divided by configured dimensions.

Confidence is configured from source authority, completeness, consistency, and recency. AI does not assign it. Official organization, university, research institution, government, filing, patent, research, and program sources have priority. Secondary public sources may supplement but not replace primary identity evidence.

NEXORA Organization Intelligence is organization-level only. It does not build personal profiles, collect personal contact details, infer sensitive personal information, or track private employees and founders. It is not investment, credit, legal, sales, or private-company intelligence.
