# NEXORA Public Privacy Notice / 公开隐私说明

Last reviewed: 2026-08-11.

NEXORA's public release candidate is a read-oriented research demo. It has no application sign-in, advertising, behavioral analytics, people profiles, or private-contact collection. The interface stores only the selected language (`en` or `zh`) in browser `localStorage`.

The evidence-first AI route processes a submitted question to produce the response and is configured with `no-store`; NEXORA does not write prompts to an application database. Do not submit credentials, personal data, confidential material, or unpublished work. External source links are governed by their operators, and the hosting platform may process IP, request, security, and operational logs under its own policy.

NEXORA 公开候选版是只读型研究演示，不设应用登录、广告、行为分析、个人画像或私人联系方式收集。界面仅在浏览器 `localStorage` 保存语言选择（`en` 或 `zh`）。

循证 AI 路由仅为生成回答而处理问题，并设置 `no-store`；NEXORA 不将提示写入应用数据库。请勿提交凭证、个人信息、机密材料或未发表研究。外部来源与托管平台日志受各自政策约束。
