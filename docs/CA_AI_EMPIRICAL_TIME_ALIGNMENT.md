# California AI Empirical Time Alignment / 加州 AI 实证时间对齐

Alignment version: `ca-ai-alignment-v1.0` (2026-08-11).

There is **no common longitudinal window across OpenAlex, NSF and BLS**. The recommended complete-year research window is 2015–2025; it applies only to the fixed OpenAlex institution frame. Cross-layer reporting uses 2025 as a reference year and remains context-only.

不存在同时覆盖 OpenAlex、NSF 与 BLS 的共同纵向时间窗。建议的完整科研年度窗口为 2015—2025 年，仅适用于固定 OpenAlex 机构框架。跨层报告以 2025 年为参考年，并仅作为背景。

| Provider | Metric | First year | Last complete year | Partial years | Update cadence | Recommended comparison window | Notes |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| OpenAlex | Annual unique AI-subfield works | 2015 | 2025 | 2026 | Ongoing snapshot refresh | 2015–2025 | Fixed 16-institution frame; 2026 is excluded from growth, CAGR, panel and paper trend comparisons. |
| NSF Award Search API | Title-qualified award count and nominal obligations | 2025 | 2025 | None in snapshot | Ongoing award-record updates | 2025 only | One agency and award year; California recipient-state slice. |
| BLS OEWS | AI-adjacent employment, LQ and mean wage | 2025 | 2025 | None | Annual | May 2025 cross-section only | One official MSA; not a statewide workforce series. |

Rules: never compare partial 2026 with complete 2025 as equivalent; never use 2026 as a CAGR endpoint; no funding/talent trend is claimed from a single cross-section.
