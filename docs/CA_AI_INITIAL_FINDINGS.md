# California AI Initial Findings

1. The selected-institution AI series is multi-year but not monotonic: 2,240 works in 2022 and 2,606 in 2025, a derived 16.3% change. This is activity, not quality.
2. The Bay Area has the largest unique-work count among the case’s selected regional institution groupings. This is not a statewide or global ranking.
3. Verified OpenAlex co-authorship connects institutions across the Bay Area, Los Angeles, San Diego, Orange County and statewide context. Co-authorship is not the whole innovation network.
4. Official policy evidence spans state adoption, procurement, transparency, workforce, health and frontier-model governance. Intended mechanism is not observed impact.
5. Seven case themes demonstrate research diversity, while unmapped topics remain outside theme subtotals.
6. Patent, capital, startup-formation, market and comparable AI-talent gaps prevent a complete commercialization assessment.

Every product finding maps to support IDs in `data/cases/california-ai/findings.ts`.
