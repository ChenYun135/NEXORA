# California AI Talent Taxonomy / 加州 AI 人才分类法

Integrated May 2025 OEWS SOCs: `15-0000`, `15-1221`, `15-1252`, `15-2051`, `15-1212`. Fields retain employment, location quotient, annual mean wage, reference period and source MSA.

Label: **AI-adjacent technical workforce / AI 邻近技术劳动力**. Do not call these records AI workers, skills supply, graduates or statewide talent. OEWS excludes self-employed workers and combines six survey panels. / 不得称为 AI 从业者、技能供给、毕业生或全州人才；OEWS 不含自雇人员，并组合六个调查面板。
