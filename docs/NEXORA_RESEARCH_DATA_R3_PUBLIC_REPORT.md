# NEXORA Research Data Expansion R3 — public report

R3 adds a reproducible, public-safe California evidence layer for 2015–2023. It activates official bulk products from the U.S. Census Bureau Business Formation Statistics and Business Dynamics Statistics, NCSES Higher Education Research and Development Survey, SBA SBIR/STTR award data, and BEA Regional Economic Accounts.

The public export contains state-year aggregates and source manifests with SHA-256 checksums. Raw archives, record-level files, unpublished research panels, model calibration material, and all paper workspaces remain private.

## What the series mean

- BFS measures business applications, including high-propensity applications. Applications are not realized startups.
- BDS measures realized firm and establishment dynamics, including firm-age-zero cohorts, establishment entry and exit, employment, job creation, and job destruction.
- HERD measures higher-education R&D expenditures by public-use source categories.
- SBIR/STTR aggregates measure public awards by program and phase. They do not measure commercialization success.
- BEA real GDP is economic context, not an innovation outcome.

California policy records are not promoted in R3 because official session archives still require record-level timing review. Publication, chaptering, effective date, implementation, and observed effect remain separate states.
