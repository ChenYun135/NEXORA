# NEXORA Current Limitations / 当前局限

As of **2026-08-10**, NEXORA is a private, Owner-only hybrid prototype. It combines a narrow verified public-data pilot with clearly labeled Demo, Derived, Composite, AI and Simulated states.

截至 **2026-08-10**，NEXORA 是仅限所有者访问的私有混合原型：使用小范围已核验公共数据试点，并明确标识演示、衍生、综合、AI 和模拟状态。

- **Production coverage / 生产覆盖：** OpenAlex research observations, World Bank country indicators, Data.gov catalog metadata, verified official organization identities, selected official policies/programs and one verified relationship. It is not globally complete.
- **Demo coverage / 演示覆盖：** Most Atlas indices, Radar momentum profiles, ecosystem relationships, organization intelligence records, policy analytical records and home KPIs remain Demo.
- **Data.gov:** Degraded; the application retains the last-known-good catalog snapshot. Catalog presence does not prove reuse rights or dataset quality.
- **USPTO:** Not configured. Production patent coverage is unavailable until a server-side credential and license review are approved.
- **OpenAlex:** Topic mappings are curated proxies, not authoritative industry classification.
- **World Bank:** Values are country-level, can lag and should not be interpreted as city or ecosystem facts.
- **NEXORA AI:** No live model provider is configured. The current workspace uses deterministic evidence-first retrieval/synthesis and labels gaps.
- **Simulator:** Partially calibrated conceptual model. Results depend on explicit assumptions and are not forecasts, causal proof or policy recommendations.
- **Privacy:** No private people profiles, contact data or personal datasets are included.
- **Advice boundary:** NEXORA does not provide investment, legal, regulatory or policy advice.
- **Localization:** Major product workflows are bilingual and the language preference persists. Long methodology documents are primarily English with bilingual summaries.
- **Accessibility:** Keyboard focus, semantic status text, reduced motion and text/table alternatives are present; advanced canvas manipulation remains more efficient on desktop.

Future flagship slot: **California AI real-data case**. This release reserves only the concept and route handoff pattern; it adds no fabricated case data and does not begin Post-Sprint 02.

The live private case now integrates research/network, BLS talent and NSF awards. It remains bounded: no statewide research census, no AI-worker count, no multi-agency funding total, no live patents, no AI-startup formation series, no reusable venture-capital series, and no causal/econometric validation. / 私有案例已接入科研/网络、BLS 人才与 NSF 奖项，但仍无全州普查、AI 从业人数、多机构资金总额、实时专利、AI 初创形成、可复用风投资列或因果/计量验证。
