# Policy Provenance / 政策来源与证据

Preferred production sources are official legislation, regulatory authorities, government agencies, official program pages, public databases, government reports, government press releases, and official institutions. Secondary sources may provide supplementary context but must not replace primary evidence for status or legal effect.

Every source record supports source name, canonical URL, source type, publisher, publication and retrieval dates, jurisdiction, document title and ID, language, time period, and notes. Evidence states remain distinct: Verified Public Source, Partial Public Evidence, Derived Classification, Demo Record, and Insufficient Evidence.

Confidence is configured from source authority, completeness, recency, cross-source consistency, and status certainty. AI does not invent confidence. Sprint 5 confidence is explicitly demo-configured.

Sprint 5 does not store policy document bodies or scrape large documents. Production summaries must distinguish source summaries from NEXORA summaries and retain provenance.
