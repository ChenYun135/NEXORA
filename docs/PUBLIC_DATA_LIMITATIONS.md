# NEXORA Public Data Limitations

- The provider catalog is broader than the set of production-ready snapshots.
- OpenAlex topic mappings are versioned proxies; publication counts do not measure innovation quality.
- HERD and award data require canonical institution identifiers and vintage-aware harmonization.
- OEWS occupation estimates and QCEW industry employment answer different questions.
- BFS applications and projected formations are not actual startups; BDS measures realized firm and establishment dynamics.
- ACS estimates require table universes and margins of error.
- BEA measures require explicit table, line code, geography, unit and vintage.
- Patent geography uses source-record addresses, never headquarters by default.
- Policy publication, program objectives, funding announcements and observed outcomes are distinct states.
- Data access does not imply unrestricted raw redistribution.
- No robust public measure of private venture capital is configured.
- Public snapshots and generic infrastructure do not disclose or represent any unpublished research design.
