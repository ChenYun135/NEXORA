# Simulation Limitations / 模拟局限

- This is a conceptual, partially calibrated model—not a forecast or decision oracle.
- Seven of nine stocks are assumption-led.
- OpenAlex publication counts are mapped-topic proxies; knowledge stock is derived from the same source.
- Patents, private capital, startup formation/exit, commercialization outcomes, talent mobility, adoption, infrastructure capacity/effect, and policy implementation/effect are unavailable.
- Country-level World Bank R&D context cannot represent metro ecosystems.
- Demo graph, health, policy-intensity, and organization-activity values are excluded from production calibration.
- Annual Euler time steps simplify within-year behavior.
- Network centrality modifies connectivity only and does not establish causality.
- One-way sensitivity omits parameter interactions; no probability distributions or confidence intervals are claimed.
- External shocks, strategic behavior, distributional impacts, and sector-specific nonlinearities are simplified or absent.

Use results for structured exploration, challenge assumptions with evidence, and do not treat small numerical differences as meaningful precision.
