# NEXORA Product Vision / 产品愿景

NEXORA maps what is emerging, explains what drives it, and helps people see what comes next. NEXORA 绘制正在崛起的未来产业，解释其驱动力，并帮助用户预见下一步。

The platform serves academic research, policy analysis, innovation intelligence, and strategic decisions. It connects industries, technologies, organizations, research, patents, regions, investment, and public policy without presenting AI interpretation as observed fact.

平台面向学术研究、政策分析、创新情报与战略决策，将产业、技术、组织、科研、专利、区域、资本与公共政策连接起来，并绝不将 AI 解读冒充为客观事实。

## Principles / 原则

- Global, scientific, premium, trustworthy / 全球化、科学、专业、可信
- Public, reusable, non-sensitive data only / 仅使用公开、可合法复用、非敏感数据
- Provenance at record level / 记录级来源追踪
- Bilingual product experience / 中英双语产品体验
