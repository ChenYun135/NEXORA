## Research question

How do research capacity, organizational structure, innovation networks, technology momentum, and public policy interact in the evolution of California's AI innovation ecosystem?

科研能力、组织结构、创新网络、技术动能与公共政策如何共同作用于加州人工智能创新生态系统的演进？

## Boundaries

- Primary geography: California; deepest evidence view: San Francisco Bay Area.
- Secondary evidence views: Los Angeles, San Diego, Orange County/Irvine, and Sacramento policy context.
- `Silicon Valley` is a semantic alias for `sf-bay-area`, not a separate administrative geography.
- Research time range is 2015–2026 where the source provides it. 2026 is incomplete; 2025 is the last complete comparison year.
- Research evidence is a reproducible 16-institution frame derived from the top 200 U.S. OpenAlex AI-activity institutions, not a census of all California research.
- No person-level profiles, private affiliations, paid databases, private funding data, or proprietary company intelligence are used.

## Secondary questions

The case examines geographic concentration, research-theme activity, organizational roles, collaboration, BLS AI-adjacent occupations, NSF awards, policy mechanisms, evidence gaps, and simulator calibration. It does not claim a global ranking, causal effect, statewide AI-worker count, market size, startup count, capital total, or live patent signal.
