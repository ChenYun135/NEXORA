# Organization Comparison Method

NEXORA intentionally avoids a single organization score. Comparison is descriptive and type-aware.

Common dimensions: entity type, regions, industries, technologies, ecosystem roles, relationship-derived connectivity, relationship diversity, and evidence coverage. Research institutions and universities add research/patent links; startups and corporations add public policy/program links; investors add technology exposure. Cross-type comparison shows the union and labels applicability.

Missing values render as unavailable, not zero. Connectivity is the configured one-hop relationship count. Diversity is distinct relationship types. Evidence coverage is the share of configured evidence dimensions with evidence IDs. Demo metrics remain labeled and are not investment, financial-attractiveness, stock-performance, prestige, or credit judgments.
