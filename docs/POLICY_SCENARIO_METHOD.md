# Policy Scenario Method / 政策情景方法

The catalog contains a baseline and nine bilingual prototype scenarios: research-led, commercialization-led, talent-led, infrastructure acceleration, balanced policy, capital-heavy, policy retrenchment, research–commercialization bridge, and supply constraint.

Baseline means “reference assumptions,” not “most likely.” Each scenario changes declared parameter overrides within reviewed ranges. Policy variables act through implementation delay, research support, infrastructure, talent attraction, commercialization, and coordination channels. Program existence is not converted into policy intensity or effect.

Up to three runs can be compared. Comparisons show conditional differences against the same baseline and context. They are not rankings of real jurisdictions and must not be interpreted as policy advice.
