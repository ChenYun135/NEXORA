# Policy Comparison Method / 政策比较方法

Jurisdiction comparison uses eight descriptive dimensions configured outside the UI: Funding Intensity, Research Support, Commercialization Support, Regulatory Activity, Standards Activity, Workforce Support, Infrastructure Support, and Public Procurement.

Sprint 5 values are explicit 0–100 demo-derived metrics. They are displayed side by side and are not combined into a total score. Missing values remain `null` and render as unavailable. No implicit weighting, ranking, or normative “best policy” conclusion is produced.

司法辖区比较仅用于展示结构化维度差异，不形成单一政策质量评分。生产指标未来必须记录输入、标准化方法、时间范围、置信度与完整来源。
