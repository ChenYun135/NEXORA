# AI Evidence Readiness for Sprint 8

AI-queryable canonical entities: sources, datasets, observations, geographies, verified organization identities, verified policy/program records and verified relationships. OpenAlex/World Bank values are observed; future trend metrics are derived; existing scores and most ecosystem relationships remain Demo. Citation-ready sources include publisher, title, canonical HTTPS URL, source date/retrieval date and claim context.

Retrieval must expose status, lineage and freshness with every fact. Stale data may be cited only with an explicit stale warning. Conflicts return all authoritative source records and resolution state. AI must refuse unsupported or person-level claims and must never convert null to zero. Facts, human-curated interpretation and future AI interpretation remain separate. Bilingual retrieval uses canonical IDs with English/Chinese labels; citations point to the original official source rather than translated prose.
