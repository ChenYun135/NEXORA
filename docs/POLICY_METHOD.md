# Policy Method / 政策智能方法

NEXORA Policy Intelligence is a neutral, evidence-oriented research module. It is not legal advice, lobbying software, compliance automation, political persuasion, partisan advocacy, campaign intelligence, or surveillance.

Sprint 5 contains 28 manually authored policy-style demo records across eight jurisdictions and nine industries. They test product structure and do not assert the existence, scope, status, amount, or effect of a real policy. Demo labels remain visible throughout the interface.

Every intelligence panel separates:

1. Observed Public Fact — the Sprint 5 demo explicitly states that no real-world fact is being asserted.
2. Derived Classification — taxonomy labels such as instrument, industry, effect, and target.
3. NEXORA Interpretation — cautious language describing how a mechanism may operate.

Conceptual pathways use “may”, “can”, or “is intended to” and do not claim proven causality. Policies are never ranked as politically good or bad, and the module does not produce a policy quality score.
