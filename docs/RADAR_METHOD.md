# Radar Method / Radar 方法

## What Radar measures / Radar 衡量什么

Radar organizes multi-category evidence about change across emerging technologies: research, patents, startups, capital, policy, talent, market readiness, technology maturity, and ecosystem activity. It interprets momentum as the direction and breadth of evidence, not as an investment recommendation.

Radar 组织新兴技术在科研、专利、创业、资本、政策、人才、市场就绪度、技术成熟度与生态活动方面的多类证据。动能表示证据的方向与广度，不构成投资建议。

## What Radar does not measure / 不衡量什么

Radar does not predict guaranteed commercial outcomes, value companies, recommend securities, score individuals, or treat AI interpretation as observed fact.

## Signal taxonomy / 信号分层

1. Observed Public Signal / 公共观测信号
2. Derived Momentum Metric / 衍生动能指标
3. NEXORA Composite Score / NEXORA 综合评分
4. AI Interpretation / AI 解读

These states are never collapsed into one another. Sprint 3 values are all explicitly demo or modeled data.

## Stage model / 阶段模型

Frontier Research → Emerging → Early Commercialization → Scaling → Established. Stage is a modeled classification in Sprint 3. Future production classification may use public research, patent, commercialization, standards, regulatory, and deployment evidence.

前沿研究 → 萌芽 → 早期商业化 → 规模化 → 成熟阶段。Sprint 3 阶段均为模型化分类；未来生产阶段可依据公开科研、专利、商业化、标准、监管与部署证据。

## Time, confidence, and uncertainty / 时间、置信度与不确定性

Interfaces support 1-year, 3-year, and 5-year windows. Demo series are illustrative rather than historical facts. Confidence is configured from completeness, source diversity, recency, and consistency; AI never invents confidence. Missing evidence remains visible. Scores use one decimal at most to avoid false precision.

## Production path / 生产路径

OpenAlex can support research observations; USPTO official data can support patent observations; World Bank, Data.gov, and official national statistics can provide context; official agencies can provide policy evidence; official universities and companies can provide public institutional records. Broad live ingestion is intentionally deferred.
