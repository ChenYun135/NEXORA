# NEXORA R5 premium product report

R5 moves the public experience from light visual refinement to a coherent premium research-product system. No unpublished research titles, research questions, equations, parameters or private analytical files are included.

## Product transformations

- Atlas: three circular hotspot tiers, restrained halos, larger map labels and a higher-contrast regional briefing.
- Radar: an orbital opportunity field with technology-family color, maturity rings, clearer labels and a quieter ranked-mover layer.
- Ecosystems: community fields, anchor/bridge/specialist hierarchy, stronger relationship paths, an orbital health profile and more legible network storytelling.
- Organizations: role constellations, circular role views and continuous industry bands replace repeated card walls.
- Policy: instrument families use a consistent six-color analytical vocabulary across landscape, timeline and comparisons.
- Data Status: providers are presented as a provenance pipeline rather than equal-weight tiles.
- NEXORA AI: answers use a briefing-first hierarchy with evidence, meaning, limitations and next action.
- Simulator: results begin with a decision brief that identifies the configured bottleneck, driver to watch and the scenario boundary.
- Typography: essential analytical labels use a 14px practical floor on desktop; method notes and mobile labels remain at least 13px.
- Responsive: analytical layouts collapse progressively at 1024px and 700px without solving density by shrinking meaningful text.

## Trust boundary

The public simulator remains a generic demonstrator. Observed, derived, simulated and interpretive states remain distinct. No private research model is exposed. Provider readiness is scope-specific and missing credentials remain honestly marked unavailable.
