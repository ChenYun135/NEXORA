# Organizations Provenance

Production source priority: official organization site; official university or research-institution page; official government or public-program source; official filing; official patent database; official research database; other public databases and releases as supplementary evidence.

Each source supports publisher, publication/retrieval dates, organization ID, relationship/activity claim, time period, derived/Demo flags, and confidence. Each claim references source IDs. A real name never verifies associated roles, relationships, metrics, signals, research, patents, policy applicability, funding, or status.

Sprint 6 uses only `organizations-demo-method`. Production source onboarding, licensing review, normalization, refresh, reconciliation, and deletion handling are deferred to Sprint 7.
