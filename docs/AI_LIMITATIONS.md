# AI Limitations

- Patent evidence is unavailable until the USPTO pipeline is operational; absence is not zero.
- Existing Radar scores are Demo and are not evidence for production answers.
- Most ecosystem and organization relationships remain Demo; verified identity alone does not prove activity.
- World Bank indicators are country-level context, not Bay Area or Shenzhen metro measurements.
- Data.gov is surfaced only according to its provider status and is not silently substituted.
- The deterministic provider summarizes templates and retrieved claims; it does not provide open-ended model synthesis.
- No arbitrary web browsing, uploaded-document retrieval, private data, or predictive investment advice is supported.
