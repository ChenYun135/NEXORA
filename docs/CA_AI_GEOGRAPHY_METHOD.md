# California AI Geography Method / 加州 AI 地理方法

OpenAlex institutions must have `geo.region=California`; individually resolved campuses map to NEXORA regions by a documented deterministic table. The 2025 BLS source area `0041940` remains the exact `san-jose-sunnyvale-santa-clara-msa` geography and only **partially overlaps** the nine-county `sf-bay-area`; the two IDs are never substituted. NSF uses awardee-state CA, retains recipient city, and does not infer award-impact geography from institution identity.

State, MSA, metro-ecosystem, awardee and performance geographies are never treated as interchangeable. / 州、MSA、都会生态、受资助地与履约地不得互换。
