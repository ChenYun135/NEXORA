# California AI Measurement Bias / 加州 AI 测量偏差

- OpenAlex coverage and algorithmic topics vary by field, language, affiliation and backfill.
- Top-200 candidate framing omits lower-activity California institutions.
- Co-authorship favors publication-visible collaboration.
- BLS occupational proxies omit self-employment and do not identify AI work.
- NSF title matching improves precision but lowers recall; one agency/year is not total funding.
- Official entity pages privilege organizations with stable public identities.

NEXORA reports these as coverage limits, not deficiencies of California. / 这些是覆盖限制，不是加州生态缺陷。
