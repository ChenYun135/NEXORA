# Organizations Taxonomy

## Entity types

STARTUP, CORPORATION, UNIVERSITY, RESEARCH_INSTITUTION, INVESTOR, GOVERNMENT_AGENCY, PUBLIC_PROGRAM, NONPROFIT, INDUSTRY_ASSOCIATION, PUBLIC_LAB, OTHER_ORGANIZATION.

## Roles

RESEARCH, TECHNOLOGY_DEVELOPMENT, COMMERCIALIZATION, MANUFACTURING, INVESTMENT, POLICY, PUBLIC_FUNDING, INFRASTRUCTURE, EDUCATION, STANDARDIZATION, SUPPLY_CHAIN, MARKET_DEVELOPMENT, ECOSYSTEM_COORDINATION.

Roles use OBSERVED_PUBLIC_CLASSIFICATION, DERIVED_ROLE, or DEMO_CONFIGURED. They describe ecosystem function, not prestige.

## Region presence

HEADQUARTERS, RESEARCH_SITE, OPERATING_SITE, PROGRAM_SITE, ECOSYSTEM_ASSOCIATION, OTHER. These meanings are not interchangeable.

## Industry relevance and status

Industry relevance: PRIMARY, SECONDARY, EMERGING. Organization status is nullable: ACTIVE, ACQUIRED, MERGED, INACTIVE, PUBLIC, PRIVATE, UNKNOWN. Startup stage is nullable: EARLY, GROWTH, SCALE, PUBLIC, ACQUIRED, UNKNOWN.

## Evidence

VERIFIED_PUBLIC_SOURCE, PARTIAL_PUBLIC_EVIDENCE, DERIVED_CLASSIFICATION, DEMO_RECORD, INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE. Entity identity separately uses VERIFIED_PUBLIC_ENTITY or DEMO_ENTITY.
