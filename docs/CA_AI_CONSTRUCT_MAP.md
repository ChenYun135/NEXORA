# California AI Construct Map / 加州 AI 构念映射

- Research capacity / 科研能力: unique OpenAlex works and complete-year trend; activity, not quality.
- Network connectivity / 网络连接: positive co-authorship pairs, degree and weighted degree within the current verified dataset.
- Talent capacity / 人才能力: BLS occupational employment and location quotients; AI-adjacent, not AI workers.
- Public funding / 公共资金: nominal NSF obligations for title-qualified 2025 awards; not statewide total or impact.
- Policy support / 政策支持: mechanism diversity of official instruments; not realized effect.
- Commercialization / 商业化: assumption only; BFS cannot identify AI startups.
- Patent activity / 专利活动: `NOT_CONFIGURED`, never zero-filled.
- Capital availability / 资本可用性: `UNAVAILABLE`, never inferred from press releases.

Cross-layer correlation is disabled because periods and geographies do not align. / 因时间与地理不对齐，跨层相关分析已禁用。
